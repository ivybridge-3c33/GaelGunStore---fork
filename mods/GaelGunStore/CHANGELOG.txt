GaelGunStore B42 Fork - Changelog
(Fork by ivybridge-3c33. Original mod by Pen-Pen Pirulin. Framework: AWCWF.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1.4.1 - 2026-07-28
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed (multiplayer, follow-ups to v1.4 -- all user-verified on a co-op
server):
- Duplicate weapon parts: attaching through the game's own right-click
  menu could complete without consuming the part item (vanilla equips the
  part into a hand first, and an in-hand item was invisible to every
  ownership check), so removing later yielded two -- the original plus a
  server-unknown ghost that could be neither used nor dropped. Ownership
  checks now see in-hand items, so the normal attach consumes the part
  correctly.
- Both sides handing back "their" copy of the same part on removal
  (another duplicate source): the client now tells the server whether the
  player already received the item, and the server discards its copy.
- The server's "already attached" answer now also consumes the loose
  inventory copy, instead of leaving it to resurrect next to the attached
  part.
- A server-side attach that has to create the part item fresh now carries
  the original condition -- a worn part no longer comes back at 100%.
- First shot fired right after a server-assisted attach was still loud:
  the suppressor state is now applied immediately instead of waiting for
  the server's sync.
- Safety nets for the rare unfindable-item case: an attach that could not
  consume the part records a debt that is settled exactly once (either
  the removal keeps the gun's copy, or a scanner consumes the loose item
  when it reappears).

v1.4 - 2026-07-28
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed (weapon attachments in MULTIPLAYER -- attach, removal, suppressors.
Single-player was never affected; all of these only showed on servers):
- Attaching a part on a server did nothing: the vanilla attach action does
  all of its work in complete(), which the engine never calls on MP. The
  attach now runs in perform() (same fix the removal got earlier) and also
  notifies the server, which attaches its own copy and syncs back.
- Removing a part on a server: the framework's detachWeaponPart silently
  does nothing unless the weapon's modData mirror already names the part --
  true for player-attached parts, never true for loot guns kitted by the
  server. Removal now seeds the mirror first, verifies the slot actually
  emptied, and no longer hands out a duplicate part while the original is
  still bolted on.
- Suppressor visibly on the gun but loud shots and an empty inspect UI:
  the two Java part reads (getAllWeaponParts vs getWeaponPart) can disagree
  about the same weapon server-side. Both server handlers now use the read
  the renderer uses, and an "already attached" answer repairs the client's
  view (mirror push) instead of being ignored.
- Clicking a part the inventory resync had just replaced failed silently,
  forever (item listed in the pane, action refused with the item
  "missing"). The attach path re-resolves the item by type and, when the
  client cannot produce it at all, asks the server to attach its own copy.
- Clicking an already-filled slot now says so instead of silently
  re-requesting the part from the server.
- Ghost attachment props stuck on the character model (drawn by nothing in
  any data store) are detected and removed on equip.
- Vanilla B42 hasScrewdriver test is inverted (carrying a BROKEN screwdriver
  blocked every attach/detach); shipped the Lua-side override for it
  (GGS_ScrewdriverFix -- was in the mod, previously missing from the repo).

v1.3 - 2026-07-19
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed (weapon attachment positions & availability):
- Browning Auto-5 (short / sawn-off): every attachment (scope, suppressor,
  grip, laser, mount, sling) was badly mispositioned and floating off the
  gun. Rebuilt the short model's attachment offsets from the correct
  full-length Browning Auto-5.
- Browning Auto-5 (full): laser/light attachment points sat back near the
  receiver; moved them out along the barrel.
- Becker Shotgun (short): buttstock sat wrong and the foregrip floated at
  the model origin -- the Grip attachment point was missing entirely. Fixed
  the stock position and added the Grip point.
- MTS-255 (short): every attachment mispositioned (scope sunk under the gun,
  grip/laser/light bunched at the receiver) and the ShellHolder recoil pad
  had nowhere to mount. Rebuilt from the full-length MTS-255 and added the
  missing recoil-pad point.
- Remington Model 8: foregrip floated below the gun, bipod floated under the
  barrel, scope sat a touch high -- repositioned all three (these older
  semi-autos shipped with un-tuned placeholder offsets).
- Remington Model 121: scope sat slightly high -- nudged down.
- Remington 870 (short): could not mount a buttstock at all -- the short was
  left out of both the stock allow-list (AWCWF_GunAllowAttachments) and the
  stock parts' mount list. Now accepts the R870 wood / SPS / Magpul stocks etc.
- QBS-09 (full & short): removed the buttstock slot (shouldn't take one).
- Finished the "no suppressor on sawn-offs" pass: dropped the sawn-off
  Becker / Remington 1100 / Sjorgen from the suppressor & choke mount lists.
- AAC Honey Badger: foregrip (Grip) position adjusted to sit right under the
  handguard.
- LVOA: bipod sat too high off the barrel -- lowered it.
- HKG28: bipod attachment was pinned at the gun's origin (floated off the
  weapon) -- positioned it under the barrel.
- ENARM Pentagun & Jackhammer: are auto shotguns but were set Single-only
  (no Auto fire mode), so when fully kitted the recoil-reduction stack broke
  the single-fire gate into an uncontrolled full-auto. Given them a proper
  Auto/Single fire mode with a throttled cyclic rate (like the AA-12), so
  auto fire is controlled.

v1.2 - 2026-07-18
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fixed:
- Attachment and magazine models not showing on weapons. The weapon-part
  render pass crashed every frame (getNumClassFields "Not in debug"), so no
  attachment/magazine models drew on ANY gun. Now renders correctly.
- Inspect / attach UI could crash to the main menu when playing without
  -debug (same getNumClassFields cause). Now guarded.
- Fire rate: "Single" fire mode firing full-auto and faster than the Auto
  mode. Caused by weapon RecoilDelay reaching zero/negative:
    * Grip "magpul_afg" recoil bonus -85 -> -8 (was driving RecoilDelay
      negative on any gun it was attached to).
    * XM8, SR47, VZ58, Wieger940 base RecoilDelay 3 -> 12 (went negative
      with any recoil-reducing attachment).
    * Added a safety clamp so no attachment combination can push a weapon's
      RecoilDelay low enough to break single-fire again.
- Ithaca M37 / JS-2000 sawn-off shotgun (ShotgunSawnoff) showed no model and
  could not fire. The item pointed at StaticModel/WeaponSprite "ShotgunSawnoff"
  which has no model definition, so the weapon could not equip/render (and an
  unequippable gun will not fire); repointed to "ShotgunSawnoff_new"
  (IthacaM37_short mesh). Firing stats were already correct.
- Several items displayed as "Base.xxx" instead of their proper names because
  the item was missing the DisplayName field (translations already existed).
  Added DisplayName to: throwable grenades M67, F1, RGD-5, Stielhandgranate,
  U108; and tools Bullet Press, Gun Cleaning Tools, Gun Disassembly & Attachment
  Tools (GunToolsKit), Oil Filter.
- M1887 shotgun: attachments (suppressor, sight, light, laser, grip) rendered
  clumped at the receiver instead of along the barrel. The M1887 model's
  attachment offsets were untuned (near origin); copied the tuned offsets from
  the sibling Remington870 model (same shared shotgun FBX / coordinate space).
- Condor sawn-off shotgun (DB_Condor_sawn): fixed reload "surrender"/hands-up
  animation (added to the forced reload-type list, like the IZH58); removed the
  suppressor slot (a sawn-off can't mount one); copied the scope/mount/laser/
  light positions from the full Condor (same receiver).
- Removed the suppressor (Canon) slot from all sawn-off / short-barrel shotguns
  (Becker, Beretta A400, Browning Auto, M1887, MTS-255, QBS09, Remington 1100,
  Remington 870 shorts, Shorty, Sjorgen, and the double-barrel sawn-offs) --
  a sawn-off has no threaded muzzle to mount one.
